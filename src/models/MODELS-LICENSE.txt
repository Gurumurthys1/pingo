Face models used by Pingo's privacy camera (both from the OpenCV Model Zoo,
https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo, mirrored at https://huggingface.co/opencv):

face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx  - YuNet face detector, MIT License
  Copyright (c) 2020 Shiqi Yu <shiqi.yu@gmail.com>

face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx - SFace face recognizer, Apache License 2.0
  Zhong, Y. et al. "SFace: Sigmoid-Constrained Hypersphere Loss for Robust Face Recognition"

Both run fully offline inside Pingo via onnxruntime-node. No images leave the PC.
